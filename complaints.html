6. Consumer withdrawal


A consumer may withdraw from a distance contract without giving a reason within 14 days of concluding it, unless the right has lawfully ended. Send a clear statement by email or post to the contacts in section 1 before the deadline. The form below is optional. Refunds are made within 14 days of receipt of withdrawal.

Services begin within this period only at the consumer's express request. If the consumer then withdraws, a proportionate payment for services actually provided may be due where the statutory information requirements have been met. For a paid service, the withdrawal right ends only after full performance and fulfilment of the legal requirements, including prior express consent and acknowledgement.

For digital content supplied without a physical medium, the right may end when supply begins only with prior express consent to early supply, express acknowledgement of losing the withdrawal right and the legally required contract confirmation. Otherwise this exception does not apply. Customisation alone does not remove withdrawal rights for our services and digital outputs. Mixed orders are assessed according to the nature of each component.

7. Defects and complaints


Deliverables must meet the agreed specifications and applicable legal requirements. Consumers retain statutory remedies; no additional commercial guarantee is offered unless expressly agreed. For one-off digital supply, consumers may report defects appearing within two years of supply; for continuous supply, we are responsible for defects during the agreed supply period. Legally required updates, including security updates, are supplied for the legally required period, separately from optional paid development.

Submit complaints by email or post using section 1. Identify the order, describe the defect and state the remedy requested. Supporting evidence is helpful but is not an unreasonable prerequisite for accepting a complaint. We acknowledge receipt and provide written confirmation of the outcome and reasons for any rejection.

Consumer service complaints are resolved within 30 days unless a longer period is agreed. For digital content and digital services, complaints are resolved within a reasonable time considering their nature and purpose, without significant inconvenience. Subject to legal conditions, remedies include correction, a proportionate price reduction or withdrawal. Describing an output as digital or customised does not remove statutory rights.
