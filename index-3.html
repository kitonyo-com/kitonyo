Good to know

Simple answers.

The details, without the tech lecture.

Is the domain included in the setup price?

The KSh 3,000 covers setup. The domain's registration or renewal fee is separate and depends on the domain ending you choose.

Are there monthly hosting fees?

A simple one-page website can often use free static hosting. If you need paid tools, email hosting, booking systems, or other services, those are priced separately.

What does the 3 hours of communication cover?

Time to discuss your design direction, preferences, content, and refinements. You can edit this description to match your exact process.

Can I expand the website later?

Yes. Additional clickable tabs or pages can be added for KSh 3,000 each.
