Data and purposes

Contact details, correspondence and supplied materials are used for enquiries and contract performance (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Billing and accounting data are retained for legal obligations (c). Necessary contractual evidence and correspondence protect legal rights, while technical logs support security, based on legitimate interests (f). Technical data may include IP address, request time, requested URL and browser information. The public website does not require login. Separate review previews use email and authentication records to restrict access.

Recipients

Cloudflare supplies hosting and security; Access authentication is used only for protected review previews. Domain email uses Seznam.cz Email Profi. GoDaddy is the domain registrar; customer domain-holder details are supplied when registration is agreed. GitHub stores source code; customer personal data must not be placed in public repositories. If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp Ireland Limited and its group also process communications under their own rules; email is available as an alternative. Necessary information may also be disclosed to accounting or legal advisers and authorised public bodies.

GoPay and Paystack payments and Fakturoid invoicing are planned but not yet connected. This notice will be updated to reflect the actual integrations before launch.

Transfers outside the EEA

Cloudflare and other global suppliers may process data outside the EEA, particularly in the USA. Transfers require GDPR safeguards such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses with necessary supplementary measures. Cloudflare publishes its data processing terms and standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about applicable safeguards.

Retention

Enquiries without a contract are retained for no more than 12 months after the last substantive communication. Contract records are normally kept during the contract and for 3 years afterwards, or as necessary for an ongoing dispute. Legally required records are retained for the applicable statutory period. Technical and access logs are retained only as necessary for security under the relevant service settings, longer only for a specific security incident.

Sources and security

We obtain information from you during enquiries and projects, and technical data through website use. Access is limited to people who need it. We do not require identity document copies or card details for a quotation. Do not include sensitive information in an ordinary enquiry.

Your rights

Subject to legal conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Any consent may be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing. We respond without undue delay, normally within one month. You may complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection. Data required for a quotation or contract are necessary to process it; without them we may be unable to provide the service. We do not use automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use visitor analytics or advertising tracking. Language and currency preferences are stored as kitonyo-language and kitonyo-currency in localStorage until changed or cleared in the browser. Cloudflare may use necessary security cookies and an Access authentication cookie to maintain the verified session (configured duration: 24 hours). Specific technical lifetimes depend on the cookie and security context. These technologies support requested functions and security, rather than advertising analytics. Preferences also work without persistent storage; authentication may require necessary cookies.
