Payments and delivery

GoPay online payments are coming soon. This page and the logo do not create an order or confirm receipt of payment.

Price and currency

Before ordering, you receive a quotation stating the scope, final price, actual payment currency and all mandatory charges. We are not registered for Czech VAT. Any mandatory foreign taxes are included in the final quotation. Currency conversions in the price list are estimates. We do not add a payment-method surcharge.

How to pay

The full price is payable in advance by the method expressly agreed in your quotation. “Coming soon” online payment buttons are disabled. Once activated, only methods and currencies actually approved in GoPay will be offered and displayed before payment. Recurring payments are not enabled.

Delivery

Delivery is electronic through a website or file link, with no shipping or electronic delivery charge. The first complete version is normally delivered within 7 calendar days after full payment and all agreed materials. Consumer early-start rules in the Terms apply. Paid domains, hosting and renewals are agreed separately before ordering.

Security and support

Data transmission on this website is secured using HTTPS (TLS). Never send card details by email or WhatsApp. Once the gateway is enabled, you will enter them directly with the payment provider. Payment support: info@kitonyo.com, +420 720 958 340.

Confirmation and refunds

Order confirmation, agreed specifications and the attached Terms and Conditions are sent by email. Once the gateway is enabled, payments will be confirmed only after verification with the provider. GoPay payments will be refunded through the gateway to the original payment instrument; any alternative must comply with gateway rules and statutory customer rights, and will not be paid out in cash.
